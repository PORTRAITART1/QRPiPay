🔒Privacy Policy - QRPiPay

Last Updated: December 12, 2025

1. Introduction

QRPiPay ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the QRPiPay application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Data:

  • Pi Network Information: Pi UID, username, email, business details
  • Payment Data: Transaction amounts, descriptions, timestamps, status
  • Usage Data: Login history, QR codes generated, analytics

Non-Personal Data:

  • Aggregated analytics
  • Performance metrics
  • Error logs

3. How We Use Your Data

  1. Service Provision: Enable payments, generate QR codes, maintain history
  2. Service Improvement: Analyze usage, fix bugs, enhance features
  3. Security: Prevent fraud, detect unauthorized access
  4. Compliance: Meet legal obligations, respond to requests
  5. Communication: Send updates, notifications, support

4. Data Retention

User Account Data: During active use

Payment Records: 7 years (tax/legal requirement)

Temporary Data: 90 days

Logs: 30 days

5. Data Security

We protect your data with:

  • 🔒 HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • 🔒 PostgreSQL encryption at rest
  • 🔒 Strong password hashing (bcrypt)
  • 🔒 Rate limiting against brute force
  • 🔒 Input validation (Zod)
  • 🔒 Audit logging
  • 🔒 Regular security updates

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • ✅ Access your personal data
  • ✅ Correct inaccurate information
  • ✅ Request deletion of your data
  • ✅ Restrict data processing
  • ✅ Export your data (portability)
  • ✅ Object to processing
  • ✅ Withdraw consent

7. Who We Share Data With

We DO share data with:

  • ✅ Pi Network API (payment processing)
  • ✅ Blockchain (transaction records)
  • ✅ Service providers (hosting, databases)
  • ✅ Legal authorities (when required)

We DO NOT share data with:

  • ❌ Advertisers or marketers
  • ❌ Data brokers
  • ❌ Unauthorized third parties

8. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For users in the European Union:

  • You have all rights under GDPR
  • We have Data Protection Officer available
  • Data transfers are safeguarded
  • Right to lodge complaint with supervisory authority

9. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

For California residents:

  • Right to know what data we collect
  • Right to delete your personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to opt-out of data sharing
  • No discrimination for exercising rights

10. Children's Privacy

Our Service is NOT for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

  3. We will detail measures taken to mitigate
