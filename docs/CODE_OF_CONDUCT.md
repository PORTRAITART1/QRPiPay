# Code of Conduct & Acceptable Use Policy - QRPiPay

**Last Updated**: December 12, 2025

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes standards for acceptable use of QRPiPay ("Service") and protects users, merchants, and the platform from legal violations and illicit activities.

## 2. Prohibited Activities (Absolute)

### 2.1 Money Laundering & Financial Crime

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Structuring transactions to avoid detection (smurfing)
- ❌ Layering funds through multiple transactions
- ❌ Placing proceeds from illegal activities
- ❌ Using QRPiPay to hide illicit income sources
- ❌ Bulk cash payments through business accounts
- ❌ Creating transactions for shell companies
- ❌ Concealing beneficial ownership
- ❌ Processing payments on behalf of sanctioned entities

**Consequence:** Immediate account termination + Legal reporting to authorities

### 2.2 Terrorism Financing & Sanctions Violations

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Payments to terrorist organizations (designated by UN, US, EU, national governments)
- ❌ Transactions with sanctioned countries (OFAC list)
- ❌ Payments to sanctioned individuals/entities
- ❌ Facilitating weapons acquisition
- ❌ Funding extremist activities
- ❌ WMD proliferation financing
- ❌ Payments to IRGC-affiliated entities
- ❌ Transactions violating export control regulations

**Consequence:** Immediate account termination + Mandatory reporting to OFAC/FinCEN

### 2.3 Drug Trafficking & Controlled Substances

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Sales of illegal drugs (heroin, cocaine, methamphetamine, etc.)
- ❌ Sales of prescription drugs without license
- ❌ Precursor chemicals for drug manufacturing
- ❌ Drug paraphernalia sales
- ❌ Marijuana (except where legal under state law - still federally illegal)
- ❌ Distribution of counterfeit medications
- ❌ Facilitating illegal drug networks

**Consequence:** Immediate account termination + Law enforcement reporting

### 2.4 Human Trafficking & Exploitation

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Sex trafficking or sexual exploitation
- ❌ Forced labor or labor trafficking
- ❌ Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- ❌ Child exploitation (any form)
- ❌ Organ trafficking
- ❌ Debt bondage
- ❌ Payments related to trafficking networks
- ❌ Facilitating servitude or forced labor

**Consequence:** Immediate account termination + Mandatory law enforcement reporting

### 2.5 Weapons & Explosives

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Illegal firearms or components
- ❌ Explosives or improvised explosive devices
- ❌ Weapons of mass destruction
- ❌ Firearms without proper licensing
- ❌ Ammunition sales (in some jurisdictions)
- ❌ Weapons modifications
- ❌ Military-grade weapons to unauthorized parties
- ❌ Cyber weapons or hacking tools (for illegal use)

**Consequence:** Immediate account termination + Law enforcement reporting

### 2.6 Fraud & Deception

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Payment fraud (chargebacks, authorization fraud)
- ❌ Identity theft
- ❌ Account takeover
- ❌ Phishing or social engineering
- ❌ Ponzi schemes or pyramid schemes
- ❌ Investment fraud
- ❌ Romance scams
- ❌ Advance fee fraud
- ❌ Credential harvesting
- ❌ False representations
- ❌ Undisclosed conflicts of interest

**Consequence:** Account termination + Civil/criminal liability

### 2.7 Intellectual Property Violations

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Counterfeiting (goods, currency, documents)
- ❌ Trademark infringement
- ❌ Copyright infringement
- ❌ Patent infringement
- ❌ Trade secret theft
- ❌ Software piracy
- ❌ Fake documentation
- ❌ Stolen goods sales

**Consequence:** Account termination + DMCA/legal action

### 2.8 Cybercrime & Hacking

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Unauthorized computer access
- ❌ Data theft or exfiltration
- ❌ Ransomware distribution
- ❌ DDoS attacks
- ❌ Malware development/distribution
- ❌ Phishing campaigns
- ❌ Credential stuffing
- ❌ Selling hacking tools
- ❌ Database breaches
- ❌ Identity fraud facilitating

**Consequence:** Account termination + Criminal prosecution

### 2.9 Environmental Crimes

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Illegal wildlife trafficking
- ❌ Endangered species sales
- ❌ Illegal logging
- ❌ Hazardous waste trafficking
- ❌ Ozone-depleting substance sales
- ❌ Illegal dumping facilitation
- ❌ Pollution from criminal activity

**Consequence:** Account termination + Reporting to environmental agencies

### 2.10 Corruption & Bribery

**STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ Bribery of officials
- ❌ Kickback schemes
- ❌ Embezzlement facilitation
- ❌ Public corruption payments
- ❌ Undisclosed payments to government officials
- ❌ Facilitation of corrupt transactions

**Consequence:** Account termination + FCPA/reporting

## 3. High-Risk Activities (Flagged for Review)

### 3.1 Activities Requiring Enhanced Due Diligence

- 🚩 Transactions above $10,000 threshold
- 🚩 Multiple rapid transactions (potential structuring)

- 🚩 High transaction velocity
- 🚩 Inconsistent beneficial ownership

- 🚩 High-risk industry involvement

## 6. OFAC & Sanctions Compliance

### 6.1 OFAC Requirements

QRPiPay complies with:

✅ **Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC)**
- Blocked Person Sanctions List (SDN)
- Specially Designated Nationals
- Consolidated Non-SDN List
- Foreign Sanctions Evaders List

✅ **Real-Time Screening**
- Name matching (fuzzy logic)
- Geographic screening
- Entity type verification
- Alias detection
- Related party analysis

✅ **Prohibited Countries**
- Iran
- North Korea (DPRK)
- Cuba (with exceptions)
- Syria
- Crimea/Donetsk/Luhansk
- Other sanctioned jurisdictions

### 6.2 Transaction Blocking

Any transaction matching OFAC lists is:
- 🛑 Automatically blocked
- 📋 Documented for 10 years
- 📞 Reported to OFAC within 10 days
- 👤 Customer notified of block
- 🔍 Never permitted to proceed

## 7. PEP (Politically Exposed Person) Screening

### 7.1 PEP Definition

Includes:
- ✅ Current heads of state/government
- ✅ Senior government officials
- ✅ Judicial officials
- ✅ Military leaders
- ✅ Central bank officials
- ✅ State-owned enterprise heads
- ✅ Family members (up to 2 degrees)
- ✅ Close associates

### 7.2 PEP Procedures

For identified PEPs:

1. ✅ Enhanced due diligence required
2. ✅ Senior management approval
3. ✅ Source of wealth verification
4. ✅ Ongoing monitoring enhanced
5. ✅ Enhanced documentation retained
6. ✅ Annual reassessment

## 8. Beneficial Ownership Disclosure

### 8.1 Requirements

All business accounts must disclose:

✅ **Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO)**
- Individual(s) with >25% ownership
- Control through voting rights
- Indirect ownership chains
- Trust beneficiaries

✅ **Verification Documents**
- Corporate registry documents
- Ownership certificates
- Trust documents
- Cap table
- Board resolutions

### 8.2 Red Flags

We investigate accounts with:
- 🚩 Undisclosed beneficial owners
- 🚩 Frequent ownership changes
- 🚩 Complex trust structures
- 🚩 Offshore structures (without clear business purpose)
- 🚩 Shell company indicators
- 🚩 Nominee shareholders

## 13. Enforcement & Consequences

### 13.1 Enforcement Tiers

**Tier 1 - Warning**
- Minor policy violations
- First-time offenses
- Immediate correction

**Tier 2 - Suspension**
- Repeated violations
- Higher-risk activities
- 30-day minimum suspension

**Tier 3 - Termination**
- Illegal activities
- Sanctions violations
- Fraud or deception
- Permanent account closure

**Tier 4 - Legal Action**
- Criminal activity
- Money laundering
- Terrorism financing
- Cooperation with law enforcement

### 13.2 Reporting Obligations

QRPiPay will report to authorities:

📞 **When Required by Law:**
- FinCEN (Suspicious Activity Reports)
- OFAC (blocked transactions)
- FBI (potential criminal activity)
- Secret Service (counterfeiting)
- ICE (human trafficking)
- DEA (drug trafficking)
- CISA (cybercrime)
- Local law enforcement

### 13.3 Account Closure Process

Upon violation:

1. Account immediately frozen
2. All transactions reviewed
3. Records preserved for 5-7 years
4. Law enforcement notified (if applicable)
5. Customer notified in writing
6. No refunds for terminated accounts
7. Cooperation with investigations
