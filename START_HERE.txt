## 🎯 WHAT IS QRPIPAY?

QRPiPay is a beautiful payment terminal for Pi Network that lets merchants:
- Generate QR codes in 10 seconds
- Accept Pi payments instantly
- Track payments in real-time
- Export data

It's built, tested, deployed, and READY FOR USERS.
